

# 📱 Telco Customer Churn Analysis — Tableau

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Telco Customer Churn Analysis** project uses Tableau to investigate customer churn patterns and identify customer segments with higher retention risk.

The analysis focuses on understanding **who is churning, which factors are associated with churn, and where retention efforts could have the greatest impact**.

The project is presented through an interactive Tableau dashboard designed for business and management analysis.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Customer churn can negatively affect revenue, customer lifetime value, and long-term business growth.

The objective of this project is to answer:

> **Which customer segments have the highest churn rates, and what factors may be contributing to customer attrition?**

---

## 🔎 Business Questions

The analysis investigates:

- What is the overall customer churn rate?
- Which contract types have the highest churn?
- Does customer tenure affect churn?
- Which internet service customers are most likely to churn?
- Which payment methods have higher churn?
- How do support and security services relate to churn?
- Are higher monthly charges associated with churn?
- Which customer segments should receive retention attention?

## 📌 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Customer Churn Overview

The dashboard provides key performance indicators including:

- Total Customers
- Churned Customers
- Churn Rate
- Retention Rate

### 2. Churn by Tenure

Customers are segmented into tenure groups to identify when churn risk is highest.

The analysis shows that newer customers have substantially higher churn than long-term customers.

### 3. Churn by Contract

Contract type is one of the strongest patterns identified in the analysis.

| Contract | Customers | Churn Rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Month-to-month | 3,875 | 42.7% |
| One year | 1,473 | 11.3% |
| Two year | 1,695 | 2.8% |

Month-to-month customers represent the highest-risk contract segment.

### 4. Churn by Internet Service

| Internet Service | Customers | Churn Rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| DSL | 2,421 | 19.0% |
| Fiber optic | 3,096 | 41.9% |
| No internet service | 1,526 | 7.4% |

Fiber optic customers show a significantly higher churn rate than DSL and customers without internet service.

### 5. Churn by Payment Method

Electronic check customers have the highest churn rate among the payment-method segments.

| Payment Method | Customers | Churn Rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Electronic check | 2,365 | 45.3% |
| Mailed check | 1,612 | 19.1% |
| Bank transfer (automatic) | 1,544 | 16.7% |
| Credit card (automatic) | 1,522 | 15.2% |

### 6. Support & Security Services

The dashboard compares churn among customers with and without services such as:

- Online Security
- Tech Support

Customers without these services show substantially higher churn rates.

## 🔎 Key Insights

### 1. Churn is concentrated among newer customers

Customers with less than 12 months of tenure have the highest churn rate at approximately **47.4%**.

### 2. Contract type is strongly associated with churn

Month-to-month customers have a **42.7% churn rate**, compared with only **2.8%** for two-year contracts.

### 3. Fiber optic customers require further investigation

Fiber optic customers have a **41.9% churn rate**, substantially higher than DSL customers.

### 4. Electronic check customers show elevated churn

Customers using electronic checks have a churn rate of approximately **45.3%**.

### 5. Support and security services are associated with lower churn

Customers with Online Security and Tech Support show substantially lower churn rates than customers without these services.

---

## 💡 Retention Opportunities

Based on the analysis, potential areas for further investigation include:

- Improve onboarding for new customers
- Encourage customers to move from month-to-month contracts
- Investigate the experience and pricing of fiber optic customers
- Examine why electronic-check customers churn at higher rates
- Promote security and technical support services
- Monitor high-value customers with elevated churn risk

These recommendations are analytical opportunities rather than assumptions about causation.
